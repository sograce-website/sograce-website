Product and network

Product and network


How do GPS, Wi-Fi, and LBS positioning work together?

Many GPS devices use GPS for outdoor positioning, Wi-Fi positioning where supported, and LBS as a cellular network-based fallback. Actual performance depends on the model, environment, network, and platform configuration.

Do Sograce devices support LTE or 4G networks?

Some Sograce devices support LTE or 4G networks. Network support is model-specific, so buyers should check frequency bands and regional requirements before selecting products from the Products page.

How should buyers confirm regional frequency compatibility?

Buyers should compare the device frequency bands with local carrier network bands in the target region. Sograce can help review model specifications, but final network suitability should be confirmed before ordering.

What SIM card is required for GPS devices?

SIM card requirements depend on the device model, network type, region, and data service. In general, the SIM should support the required cellular network and data service for device communication.

What GPS accuracy can be expected?

GPS accuracy varies by device, antenna design, satellite signal, installation position, weather, buildings, and operating environment. Outdoor open-sky positioning is usually more reliable than obstructed environments.

Can GPS devices locate indoors?

Indoor positioning depends on the model and available signals. GPS may be weak indoors, while Wi-Fi or LBS positioning may help where supported. Accuracy can vary significantly by building and network environment.

How long does the battery last?

Battery life depends on battery capacity, reporting interval, network signal, calling, alerts, sensors, screen use, and power-saving settings. Buyers should evaluate battery expectations by model and real use case.

Are the GPS devices waterproof?

Waterproof ratings vary by model. Buyers should check the specific product specification and confirm whether the rating is suitable for rain, daily wear, pet use, or outdoor work conditions.

How does the SOS button work?

The SOS button can trigger an emergency event on supported devices. The alert behavior may include app notification, platform event, location reporting, or calling, depending on the model, firmware, SIM service, and platform settings.

Do devices support two-way calling?

Two-way calling is available on selected GPS watches and SOS devices. It requires compatible hardware, firmware, SIM voice service where needed, and correct configuration.

Do Sograce devices support fall detection?

Fall detection is available on selected models. It depends on sensor hardware, firmware algorithm, wearing method, and scenario, so it should be tested for the target elderly care or safety use case.

What are geofence alerts?

Geofence alerts notify users when a device enters or leaves a defined area on supported platforms. Actual alert behavior depends on positioning quality, reporting interval, app settings, and platform configuration.

Can users view historical routes?

Historical route playback is available on supported devices and platforms. Route quality depends on positioning data, reporting frequency, network connection, and data storage settings.

Do devices support low battery alerts?

Low battery alerts are supported by many GPS devices, but behavior depends on model firmware, battery level settings, app notifications, and platform configuration.

What health monitoring features are available?

Health monitoring features vary by model. Some watches may include heart rate, blood oxygen, temperature, blood pressure, sleep, step counting, fall detection, or reminders. Buyers should confirm supported features for each product.
