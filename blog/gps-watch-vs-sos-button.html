GPS Watch vs Traditional SOS Button

Traditional SOS buttons provide emergency calls, but GPS watches add live location, geofence alerts, fall detection, health monitoring and mobile APP access. This makes GPS watches more suitable for modern elderly care and remote caregiver monitoring.

Key Features to Consider

Real-Time Location

Reliable GPS, Wi-Fi and cellular positioning helps caregivers locate users when needed.

SOS Alerts

A dedicated SOS button can notify emergency contacts and platform users quickly.

Geofence & APP

Safe zone alerts and mobile APP monitoring help families and businesses manage devices.
