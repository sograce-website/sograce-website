4G Elderly GPS Health Watch

G45PRO 4G Elderly GPS Health Watch

GPS/Wi-Fi/LBS senior safety watch with SOS, two-way calls, health monitoring and Globe GPS Tracker support.

G45PRO is a 4G GPS health watch for elderly care and family safety projects. It combines GPS, Wi-Fi, LBS and A-GPS positioning with two-way calling, geofence notifications, one-month history query, low battery alerts and remote monitoring through Globe GPS Tracker.
