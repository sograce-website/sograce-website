Main Features

4G GPS Smart Watch for Elderly Safety


4G LTE Connectivity

Reliable mobile connectivity for senior GPS smart watch tracking, family communication and emergency assistance.

GPS + WiFi + LBS positioning

Multi-mode positioning helps caregivers view location, geofence alerts and historical route tracking from the APP or PC platform.

SOS Emergency Button

A dedicated SOS button supports fast emergency assistance for elderly users, family safety monitoring and care service teams.

Voice and Video Calling

Two-way voice calling and video calling keep seniors connected with family members and remote caregivers.

Remote Care Management

Remote monitoring, low battery reminder and remote shutdown help families and service providers manage devices efficiently.

APP, PC Platform and API

N50 works with Globe GPS Tracker APP, the Sograce GPS platform and API integration for GPS watch OEM projects.
