2-in-1 Senior GPS Safety Device

G41 2-in-1 4G GPS Smartwatch & SOS Pendant

Wearable GPS Safety Device for Seniors - Use as a Smartwatch or SOS Pendant

G41 is a wearable 4G GPS safety device for seniors that can be worn as a smartwatch or used as an SOS pendant. It supports emergency calling, fall detection, two-way communication, location features and remote management for elderly care projects.

2-in-1 Wearable Design

The same G41 device can support wrist-worn use with a silicone wristband or pendant-style safety use, helping elderly care teams choose the wearing method that fits each user.
