G40 4G GPS Kids Smartwatch with Video Calling

4G Kids Safety Watch with GPS/Wi-Fi/LBS Positioning, Video Calling and Geofence

G40 is a children's GPS safety smartwatch for family location and communication projects. It combines 4G video calling, GPS, Wi-Fi, LBS and A-GPS positioning, SOS alerts, geofence notifications, voice chat and one-month trace inquiry in a compact IPX7 kids watch design.

Safety, location and family communication

  • GPS, Wi-Fi, LBS and A-GPS positioning
  • Wi-Fi 3-50 m, GPS 3-10 m, LBS 100-500 m stated range
  • Multiple geofences
  • Entry and exit safety-area notifications
  • Location tracking and track playback
  • One-month activity/location trace inquiry
  • 4G HD video calling through mobile app
  • Two-way voice chat
  • Group voice chat
  • Phone book up to 15 family/friend contacts
  • Speed dial
  • Remote monitor / remote listening function
  • SOS alarm
  • Low battery alarm and battery status monitoring
  • Automatic network time synchronization
  • G-sensor and pedometer
  • IPX7 water resistance
  • Android and iOS supported
